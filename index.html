FOR THE COMMITTED

Permanent Member

You're in for the long haul. Full patch, priority on every ride, club merch, voting rights on routes and events, and a seat at every bonfire.

  • All group rides, no cap
  • Official club kit & patch
  • Say in club decisions
  • Long-distance tour priority
FOR THE FREE SPIRITS

Casual Rider

Ride when the road calls you. Join weekend runs and open events, no commitment, all the good vibes.

  • Open invite to public rides
  • Community WhatsApp/Telegram
  • Upgrade to permanent anytime
  • Zero pressure, all throttle
